Clear, clean communication is the whole system

Every step in this SOP is a handoff — editor to content manager, content manager back to editor, content manager to client. The pipeline only moves as fast as the notes are clear. If a cut, a hook line, or a revision isn't obvious, say so immediately rather than guessing. A quick clarifying message costs nothing; a wrong assumption costs a full re-edit.

Slack is the main channel for this — not WhatsApp. Farouk will be onboarded into Slack, along with the rest of his logins, shortly.

01

Purpose & Principles

This SOP exists so every reel that leaves the edit bay meets the same bar, regardless of who's watching it first.

  • Consistent — same visual language, same caption style, every time.
  • On-brand — terracotta and white only, SF Pro, clean and intentional.
  • Punchy — short form must hook fast and hold. Every second earns its place.
  • Submittable — the editor delivers the cut, the reviewer writes the hook title, nothing goes live without full sign-off.
02

03

Source Material

Reels are sourced and organized on the team board. They'll be one of three types — match the edit approach to the source.

  • Dedicated reel footage — filmed specifically for short form. Already framed for vertical.
  • Long form pull — a moment or sequence extracted from a long form video. Needs reframing and tighter cutting.
  • Call footage — pulled from a real sales call or review session. Needs reframing and tighter cutting.

Colour & Brand

Three colours only. Nothing else touches the frame.

#FFFFFF
White
#0A0A0A
Black
#E25435
Terracotta
  • Terracotta is reserved for caption highlights and branding elements — never used as a full-frame background or overused as decoration.
  • Any motion graphics or lower-thirds must align to this exact three-colour scheme.
08

Pacing & Cutting

  • Cut all silences, filler words, and dead air tightly.
  • The first three seconds must be the strongest moment in the clip — if the opening doesn't hook, reorder.
  • Every word should fight for its place. If removing it doesn't break the meaning, remove it.
  • Do not add music unless the reviewer specifies. If music is used, it sits well underneath the speaker's voice and never competes with it.
09

Export & Delivery

  • Aspect ratio — 9:16 vertical, always. Most footage will already be filmed vertical; if the source is horizontal, letterbox it inside the same 9:16 canvas — hook above, captions below (see Section 05).
  • Resolution — 1080×1920.
  • Frame rate — match source footage.
  • Delivery — via Timeliner only. Upload to the exact item that's already set up in the content pipeline for that reel — don't create a new entry. Label it with the creator's name, reel number, and a one-line description of the clip content.
  • Hook title — deliver with none in place. It's added only after the reviewer's pass.
