J12 Consulting × Eli

The gameplan
for the next 6 months.

A working overview of the concepts, timeline, and deliverables behind our partnership — designed to grow the brand without taking from his life or work.

01 — Concepts
The Foundation
01
Brand Architecture
Eli's brand will be built around the intersection of lifestyle, sales, philosophy, and the gym — not a boxed-in niche, but a passionate, lived-in point of view. Positioning is left open on purpose: it will emerge from what he's genuinely drawn to, not a category forced on him upfront.
02
Pillars & Packaging
We'll develop a custom Pillar and Packaging system for Eli, modeled on our internal frameworks but shaped entirely to his voice and world. Pillars define his content types, Packaging defines the formats and settings — giving every video a clear category and a repeatable structure to produce against.
03
Loyal Brand Over Monetization
We're not pushing for monetization right now — the focus is building a loyal, genuinely engaged brand. Trust and audience come first; the monetization conversation gets revisited once we're working from real data.
04
Audience Discovery
Since we're not niching him down, content across lifestyle, sales, philosophy, and the gym will be tested deliberately in this phase to see what he's most passionate about and what pulls real engagement. Audience and direction get built together — not assumed.
02 — Timeline
First 3 Months
Month One
Foundation
Define pillars & packaging, set up systems (filming, editing pipeline, SOPs), begin consistent posting, editor onboarded to his team.
Month Two
Audience Testing
Push volume across pillars, track what resonates, start narrowing tone and direction based on real data.
Month Three
Review & Plan Forward
Assess what's working, refine packaging, and use the data and feedback from these three months to decide direction and scope for what's next.
"The next three months will be shaped by what we learn in these first three."
