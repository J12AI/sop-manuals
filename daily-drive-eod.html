Daily Drive + End of Day
Protocol

We move as a unit or we don't move at all. Lack of communication and misalignment on tasks is the #1 thing slowing us down. This fixes that — but only if everyone runs it the same way, every day, no exceptions.

Intention
Why this exists

These Daily Drives and EODs are massively important. They're what allows the entire team to work together and move as a single unit instead of ten people pulling in ten directions. The Daily Drive is an exact description of what you're working on for the day — not a vague gesture at it. The EOD is the honest record of what actually happened. Together, they're how the team stays aligned without needing a meeting to figure out who's doing what.

The cadence

Every morning, before you start work: your Daily Drive is due before 8:00am EST. Every evening, once work wraps: your EOD is due before 8:00pm EST. Same channel, every day, no exceptions.

The Rule
Daily Drive Posted before 8:00am EST
End of Day Posted before 8:00pm EST
Location One channel — DDs and EODs live together
Format One message per person, not a thread reply
Read before you write

Before you post your own Daily Drive, read everyone else's. This channel only works as an alignment tool if everyone actually knows what everyone else is doing that day. Posting your own without reading the room defeats the entire point.

Comment on each other's posts

Reading isn't passive. If someone's Daily Drive or EOD touches something you're also working on, overlaps with your task, or you have a note or feedback on it — comment directly under their post. That's where real alignment happens: not just two people posting side by side, but the team actually talking to each other about the work.

☀ Daily Drive · Before 8am EST
What I'm doing The main things you're working on today.
What I'm going to finish Specific, checkable — not "make progress on X."
What I need from the team If it's on someone specific — new asset, clarity, a decision — @tag them by name. Don't leave it vague, and don't bury it here if it's urgent — say it in the channel directly too. Be specific, so leadership knows what to assign or extend accordingly. If nothing's needed, write "Nothing — clear runway."

Standing Rules
Tag directly, don't wait

If something you need — or something breaking down in communication — involves a specific person, @tag them the moment you notice it. Don't wait for the next DD or EOD to surface it. The whole point of this channel is speed of response, not a daily log nobody acts on until the next cycle.

Content Meetings — Mon / Wed / Fri

This channel is the source of record for those meetings. We bring the week's Daily Drives and EODs into Monday, Wednesday, and Friday to check alignment, catch what slipped, and course-correct in real time — not from memory, from what's actually been posted here.

Example
Daily Drive — Joshua
What I'm doing: 5 new videos being scripted, content meeting with Jack, then film those 5 videos + recording the long form coaching call
What I'm going to finish: All tasks outlined above, completed and ready for review by end of day
What I need from the team: Clear communication from the editors on what's getting done today and what won't
EOD — Joshua
What got done: All tasks recorded and uploaded into Timeliner with notes — @Abdull @Jack @Fortune
1 piece of feedback: Use the exact subtitle notes/SOPs we created — that way revisions go faster. @Abdull, I'll meet with you tomorrow at 10:00am EST at Volv.
Personal win: Amazing gym workout today, completed all my daily tasks
